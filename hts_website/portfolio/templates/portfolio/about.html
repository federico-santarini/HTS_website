The Holistic Technology Salon is an invitation to co-create alternative futures through multidimensional conversations. In a series of community encounters we will use different types of knowledge – rooted in corporeal and mystical practices – to weave new, holistic ways of being with technology. In a participatory setting we will explore how to integrate eco- and carecentric perspectives into our relationship with contemporary and future technologies through direct experience.
